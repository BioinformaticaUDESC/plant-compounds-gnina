# Restinga GNINA V4: target expansion and reproducibility record

## Scope

Stage A expanded the study to six systems: MAO-A/2Z5X, MAO-B/2V61, soluble epoxide hydrolase/3ANS, KEAP1/4XMB, EGFR/1M17, and *E. coli* GyrB/1KZN. Stage B added PARP1/7KK4 and *S. aureus* tyrosyl-tRNA synthetase/1JIL. Stage B was defined after the six initial controls had been observed but before its own results were known; it is documented as an exploratory expansion and does not override failed controls.

COX-2, acetylcholinesterase, CB2, and 5-LOX are retained as V3 records and were not recalculated. See the technical PDF and `reports/RESULTADOS_V4.md` for complete results, including all systems that did not satisfy the criterion.

## Library and boundaries of inference

The curated library contains 40 literature-derived compounds, 51 declared stereochemical structures, and 59 verified conformational inputs. All inputs match their recorded hashes. The historical stereochemically divergent conformer and a redundant corrected caryophyllene-oxide diagnostic are not part of the V4 primary library.

No chemical analysis of project samples was provided. Substance–plant–matrix relationships are bibliographic. *Campomanesia littoralis* remains without a sufficiently confirmed nominal chemical assignment; *Eugenia catharinae* is represented by extract comparators while a complete essential-oil composition remains pending.

Docking does not demonstrate mechanism, inhibition, agonism, selectivity, efficacy, ADMET properties, toxicity, or safety. KEAP1 docking is not an antioxidant assay; MAO docking does not demonstrate antidepressant activity; PARP1 or EGFR docking does not demonstrate selective cytotoxicity; bacterial-target docking does not establish MIC or bactericidal activity. Scores across proteins are not a common affinity scale.

## Prespecified procedures

GNINA 1.3.3 was identified by the official SHA-256 fingerprint and executed on CPU. The protocol used CNN `fast` with `rescore`, Energy as the primary ranking, exhaustiveness 16, up to 20 poses per input, four CPUs per process, and at most two concurrent GNINA processes.

Native-ligand redocking used seeds 101, 202, 303, 404, and 505. A system was accepted only when the first Energy-ranked pose had a symmetry-aware heavy-atom RMSD ≤2 Å in at least four of five seeds. RMSD was evaluated in the fixed receptor reference frame, without an independent ligand alignment. Identical-reference, 10 Å translation, and atom-reordering tests were used to check the implementation. Best sampled pose and CNN-based reordering within the exported set are secondary diagnostics and do not alter the primary criterion.

Candidate docking was conducted only for accepted systems, using all 59 inputs and seeds 42, 142, and 242 under the same parameters. These runs are repeated searches, not biological replicates. Medians and ranges describe sensitivity to search, stereochemistry, and conformer selection; they do not support statistical inference about potency. The CNN model is the same as in V3 and should not be compared numerically with earlier deliveries that used a different ensemble.

Docking boxes were defined from experimental ligand coordinates. Independent redocking inputs were generated by ETKDGv3/MMFF with 64 attempts and without retaining native coordinates; chemical identity was rechecked after three-dimensional SDF reading. The recorded charge hypotheses are harminium (+1), zwitterionic SB284485 (amine +1/carboxylate −1; net zero), and the neutral CCD state for other controls. No population-level pKa or microstate prediction was performed.

## Structural preparation and limitations

Deposited heavy-atom coordinates were retained; hydrogens were modelled at pH 7.4. No heavy-atom relaxation or loop rebuilding was performed. Incomplete side chains were completed only when their existing atoms were ≥8 Å from the ligand. Relevant distances and residues are documented in the audit records.

Oxidised FAD was retained in MAO-A and MAO-B, with dianionic phosphates. The deposited Cys-SG/C8M linkage was preserved, without an artificial SG hydrogen; deposited FAD heavy atoms were retained. No electronic/redox or membrane model was used. Waters were removed and no sensitivity analysis for this choice was performed.

GyrB retains missing segments 82A–D and 101–115, with flanks approximately 5.9 Å from the ligand. TyrS is a catalytic construct with 323/420 residues modelled; the biological-assembly partner is at least 9.91 Å from the ligand. tRNA and dimer dynamics were not modelled. Prepared-PDB numbering, including insertions, is retained without automated UniProt conversion.

KEAP1/4L7B was not used because of R354D, and 6HWS was not used because Arg380 lacked CZ/NH1/NH2 near the ligand. Human 4XMB, without declared mutations or incomplete nearby side chains, was selected. SAG/2V5Z has CCD imine connectivity despite its safinamide synonym; C18/2V61 was selected while retaining its deposited chemistry. Excluded preliminary preparations are not counted as simulated controls.

## Audit, contents, and reproduction

Audits evaluate identity, finite coordinates, non-metal contacts <2 Å, distance/van-der-Waals-radius ratios <0.70, positive energy, box escape >0.1 Å, mapped bond changes >0.15 Å, and serious intramolecular overlap. Metals are recorded separately. Absence of an alert does not establish a correct pose; proximity ≤4 Å does not establish a hydrogen bond or mechanism.

- `reports/`: technical PDF, synthesis, PNG/SVG figures, and full compound, structure, input, seed, and contact tables.
- `targets/`: receptors, native references, independent inputs, boxes, and audit materials.
- `results/`: new poses, commands, logs, hashes, failed controls, and audits.
- `baseline_v3/`: earlier synthesised records with verified provenance and integrity.
- `previous_delivery/`: the original V3 package, including documented quarantine material.
- `prepared/`, `structures.csv`, and `docking_inputs.csv`: curated library records.
- `raw/`: RCSB/CCD sources and excluded structural alternatives.
- `selection_plan.json` / `protocol.json`: stage A; `selection_plan_round_b.json` / `protocol_round_b.json`: stage B.
- `target_rationale.json`: hypotheses, limitations, and primary sources; plant-source records are in `literature_sources.json` and `literature_species_links.csv`.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: delivery inventory.

To prepare a CPU environment, extract to the home directory and run:

```bash
cd "$HOME/Restinga_GNINA_Alvos_V4"
INSTALL_REPORT=1 bash 00_preparar_ambiente.sh
```

The binary and virtual environment are not included. Installation downloads several GB of software but does not install drivers, alter the kernel, require sudo, or require a GPU.

For a clean rerun, preserve the delivery and create a new destination:

```bash
.venv/bin/python prepare_clean_reproduction_v4.py \
  --destination "$HOME/Restinga_GNINA_V4_Reproducao_Limpa"
```

In the new directory, run `run_v4.py all`, `run_v4_round_b.py all`, `audit_v4.py`, `audit_redocking_v4.py`, `synthesize_v4.py`, `export_views_v4.py`, and `build_report_v4.py` with the recorded GNINA executable. `--resume` reuses only outputs with compatible hashes and parameters. To rebuild tables from deposited poses, use `audit_v4.py --reuse-audited`, then `synthesize_v4.py` and `build_report_v4.py`. Prepared files retain the system definition; repeating hydrogen preparation can vary numerically. Do not delete the deposited delivery to recalculate results.

Candidate prioritisation requires integration of chemical evidence, voucher and matrix confirmation, and target-appropriate experimental assays. All recorded V4 simulations were executed in this CPU environment, not on Tesla V100 GPUs or a local workstation.
